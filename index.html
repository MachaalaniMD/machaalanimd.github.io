ABOUT THE COLLECTION

Good tools make room
for better thinking.

I’m Marc Machaalani. I’m interested in the places where medicine and software meet—especially the everyday work of finding information, making sense of it, and keeping its source in view.

I started building these tools to explore a simple question: can a thoughtful interface make useful medical knowledge easier to reach? Sage and Folio approach that question differently. One connects guidelines to clinical questions; the other gives a compact manual a more navigable form.

The aim is practical: less friction, clearer context, and more room for the judgment that remains with the clinician.

A note on use

These are independent educational projects, not validated clinical systems. Sage explains its scope, privacy model and validation limits on its home page. Folio preserves the dated source edition and identifies omitted local material; it is not an updated or institution-endorsed manual.
