# Anticoagulation Agents
> Doses, switching and reversal for every anticoagulant in one place. DOACs are first line for most AF and VTE; warfarin for mechanical valves, moderate–severe mitral stenosis and high-risk APLS; heparin when you need something short-acting or kidney-independent. {ASH: Blood Adv 2018;2:3257; CHEST: Chest 2021;160:2247; AHA: JACC 2020;143:e72}

## DOACs at a glance
| Drug | Target | Half-life | Renal clearance | AF dose | VTE dose | Prophylaxis |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Apixaban** | Xa | 12 h | 25% (usable in ESRD) | 5 mg BID; **2.5 mg BID if 2 of 3:** Cr ≥1.5, weight ≤60 kg, age ≥80 (some use 2.5 mg if CrCl 15–29) {NEJM 2011;365:981} | 10 mg BID × 7 days, then 5 mg BID; after 6 months consider 2.5 mg BID {NEJM 2013;369:799; NEJM 2013;368:699} | 2.5 mg BID {NEJM 2009;361:594} |
| **Rivaroxaban** | Xa | 5–9 h (11–13 h older adults) | 66% | 20 mg daily with food if GFR >50; 15 mg if 15–50 {ROCKET AF, NEJM 2011;365:883} | 15 mg BID × 21 days, then 20 mg daily; after 6 months consider 10 mg daily {NEJM 2010;363:2499; NEJM 2012;366:1287; NEJM 2017;376:1211} | 10 mg daily {NEJM 2013;368:513} |
| **Edoxaban** | Xa | 10–14 h | 50% | 60 mg daily; 30 mg if CrCl 15–50 or weight ≤60 kg; **don't use if CrCl >95** {NEJM 2013;369:2093} | 60 mg daily after 5 days of heparin; 30 mg if CrCl 15–50, ≤60 kg, or on a P-gp inhibitor {NEJM 2013;369:1406} | |
| **Dabigatran** | IIa (thrombin) | 12–17 h | 80% | 150 mg BID if GFR >30; 75 mg BID if 15–30 (some use 110 mg BID if bleeding risk is high) {RE-LY, NEJM 2009;361:1139} | 150 mg BID after 5 days of heparin {RE-COVER, NEJM 2009;361:2342} | 110 mg once, then 220 mg daily {Thromb Haemost 2011;106:721} |

- **Interactions:** rivaroxaban, edoxaban and dabigatran are P-gp substrates; avoid rivaroxaban with combined strong CYP3A4 + P-gp inhibitors.
- **Dabigatran:** dyspepsia; possibly more coronary events.

### Switching
| From → to | How |
|---|---|
| **DOAC → heparin** | Start when the next DOAC dose is due (dabigatran: 12 h after the last dose, 24 h if CrCl <30) |
| **LMWH/fondaparinux → DOAC** | Start within 0–2 h of the next scheduled dose |
| **UFH drip → DOAC** | Start when the drip stops (edoxaban: 4 h after) |
| **Warfarin → DOAC** | Stop warfarin; start rivaroxaban when INR <3, apixaban or dabigatran when INR <2, edoxaban when INR ≤2.5 |
| **DOAC → warfarin** | Rivaroxaban/apixaban: stop, start warfarin with a parenteral bridge until INR is therapeutic. Edoxaban: halve the dose, start warfarin, stop edoxaban at INR ≥2. Dabigatran: start warfarin 3 days before stopping if CrCl ≥50, 2 days if 31–50, 1 day if 15–30. All DOACs raise the INR {J Thromb Thrombolysis 2016;41:206} |
| **DOAC → DOAC** | Start when the next dose is due |

### Reversal (life-threatening bleeding)
- **Xa inhibitors:** andexanet alfa (bolus, then 2 h infusion) {NEJM 2015;373:2413; NEJM 2019;380:1326}, or 4-factor PCC. Not dialyzable (protein-bound).
- **Dabigatran:** idarucizumab 5 g {RE-VERSE, NEJM 2017;377:431}; dialyzable.

## Warfarin
Vitamin K antagonist (factors II, VII, IX, X and proteins C and S). Half-life ~40 h (variable); many CYP2C9 interactions.
- **Start:** 5 mg daily × 2 days (2.5 mg if frail, heart failure, kidney or liver disease; consider 7.5 mg if BMI >40)
- **INR lags ~48 h** behind a dose change. Adjust by a nomogram:
| INR | Weekly dose |
|---|---|
| Below goal | Increase 5–15% (or a booster dose) |
| At goal | No change |
| 0.1–1.0 above goal | Decrease 5–15%; consider holding a dose |
| >1 above goal | Hold until at goal, then restart 10–20% lower |

- **Overlapping a direct thrombin inhibitor** (argatroban raises the INR): use chromogenic factor X, goal 20–40%.
- **Switching to heparin:** start IV without bolus once INR <2.

### Reversal
| Situation | Do |
|---|---|
| **Life-threatening or intracranial bleeding** | Vitamin K 5–10 mg IV over 10–20 min **plus** 4-factor PCC over 10 min if INR >2 (≥1.5 in ICH): INR 2–4 → 25 U/kg (max 2500); 4–6 → 35 U/kg (max 3500); >6 → 50 U/kg (max 5000). No PCC → 2 units FFP rapidly |
| **INR >10, not bleeding** | Vitamin K 2.5–5 mg PO |
| **INR 4.5–10, not bleeding** | Vitamin K 1–2.5 mg PO if bleeding risk is high; otherwise hold |
| **INR <4.5, not bleeding** | Hold warfarin; no reversal |

## Parenteral agents

|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Unfractionated heparin** (ATIII → Xa and IIa; half-life 60–90 min) | ACS: 60 U/kg → 12 U/kg/h, PTT 63–83. VTE: 80 U/kg → 18 U/kg/h, PTT 70–100. Prophylaxis: 5,000 U SC q8–12h | PTT; anti-Xa 0.3–0.7 if baseline PTT is high or doses are large; ACT at very high doses | To LMWH: give LMWH and stop UFH together. Preferred with CrCl <30, around procedures, poor absorption, pregnancy |
| **Enoxaparin** (LMWH; Xa >> IIa; half-life 4.5–7 h) | ACS/VTE: 1 mg/kg BID (daily if GFR <30). Prophylaxis: 40 mg daily (30 mg BID if very high risk; +30% if BMI ≥40; 30 mg daily if GFR <30) | Not routine; anti-Xa 4 h after the 4th dose, goal 0.5–1.0 | To UFH: stop LMWH, start UFH without bolus 1–2 h before the next LMWH dose. Accumulates in kidney failure |
| **Fondaparinux** (Xa only; half-life 17–21 h) | VTE: <50 kg 5 mg, 50–100 kg 7.5 mg, >100 kg 10 mg daily. To UFH: start UFH (no bolus) 1–2 h before the next dose. From UFH: start 1 h after stopping | **No reversal agent.** Raises aPTT at therapeutic doses |
| **Argatroban** (direct thrombin inhibitor; half-life 45 min) | HIT: 1–2 mcg/kg/min | PTT 1.5–3× baseline | To warfarin: stop once chromogenic factor X is 20–40% | No reversal agent. Caution in critical illness, heart failure, liver disease |
