# Vasopressors & Inotropes
> Start pressors when MAP stays <65 (or SBP falls >30) with organ dysfunction despite adequate volume. Norepinephrine first for almost every shock; add vasopressin in septic shock; add an inotrope when the heart is the problem. {Intensive Care Med 2019;45:1503}

## Starting and running pressors
- **When:** SBP down >30 or MAP <65 **with** organ dysfunction, despite optimized volume. Titrate to MAP, urine output and mentation. Responsiveness can wane over time.
- **Access:** central (CVC or IO) is ideal. Peripheral norepinephrine (<15 mcg/min) or phenylephrine (<150 mcg/min) is acceptable for <72 h through **two PIVs ≤20 G**, in the antecubital fossa or higher, with blood return; check the site q2h.
- **Extravasation:** stop, aspirate what you can through the catheter, inject **phentolamine 5–10 mg in 10 mL NS** into the area, apply a dry warm compress.
- Subcutaneous drugs (e.g. heparin, insulin) absorb less reliably during vasoconstriction.

## Which drug
| Drug | How it works | Use it for | Watch for | Dose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Norepinephrine** (levo) | α1 > β1: ↑↑SVR, ↑CO | **First line:** septic, cardiogenic, hypovolemic shock | Arrhythmia, worse LVOT obstruction, digital ischemia, hyperglycemia, immunosuppression {AJRCCM 2020;202:830} | Start 2–12 mcg/min (0.1–0.15 mcg/kg/min); max 35–100 mcg/min (0.75 mcg/kg/min); peripheral <10 mcg/min |
| **Vasopressin** (vaso) | V1: ↑SVR; V2: water retention | **Second in septic shock** (add at norepinephrine 5–15 mcg/min); anaphylaxis (second); pulmonary HTN/RV failure; hepatorenal syndrome | Digital, coronary, mesenteric ischemia (especially high dose); hyponatremia | Fixed 0.04 U/min; 0.08 U/min only as salvage |
| **Epinephrine** (epi) | Low dose β1 > β2 > α1: ↑CO. High dose α1 > β: ↑CO and ↑SVR | **First in ACLS and anaphylaxis;** symptomatic bradycardia (second); septic shock; bronchospasm | Tachycardia, arrhythmia, worse LVOT obstruction, ischemia, high lactate, hyperglycemia | Low 1–4 mcg/min; high 5–35 mcg/min |
| **Phenylephrine** (neo) | Pure α1: ↑↑SVR | Septic shock when norepinephrine causes marked tachycardia, CO is high with very low BP, or a third agent is needed; AF with RVR, HOCM, AS | Reflex bradycardia, lower CO, higher PA pressure, digital ischemia | Start 50–180 mcg/min (0.5–2 mcg/kg/min); max 360–1000 mcg/min (6 mcg/kg/min); peripheral <150 mcg/min |
| **Angiotensin II** | AT1 receptor: ↑SVR | Refractory distributive shock; vasoplegia after bypass or MCS | Digital, myocardial, gut ischemia; possible VTE {NEJM 2017;377:419} | Start 5–10 ng/kg/min; max 80 ng/kg/min |
| **Dopamine** (dopa) | Low dose D1 > β1; medium β1 > D1; high α1 | Symptomatic bradycardia; septic shock with bradycardia. **Higher mortality than norepinephrine** in septic {CCM 2017;45:486} and cardiogenic shock {NEJM 2010;362:779} | Tachyarrhythmia, ischemia, hypotension (low dose), higher PCWP and shunt, hyperglycemia | Low 1–2, medium 5–10, high 10–50 mcg/kg/min |
| **Methylene blue** | Blocks NO/cGMP: ↑SVR | Refractory vasoplegia (distributive shock, after bypass), amlodipine or metformin overdose, methemoglobinemia | Falsely low SpO2, arrhythmias, higher PVR (avoid in severe pulmonary HTN), hemolysis, **contraindicated in G6PD deficiency**, serotonin syndrome | 1–2 mg/kg; max 5 mg/kg |
| **Hydroxocobalamin** (Cyanokit) | IV B12: inhibits NO synthase, modifies H2S | Vasoplegia refractory to (or unable to take) methylene blue | Red urine; interferes with many lab assays {Clin Biochem 2021;91:31} | 5 g over 15 min; max 30 g/24 h |
| **Dobutamine** (inodilator) | β1, β2 > α1: ↑CO, ↓SVR | Cardiogenic shock; sepsis with low EF (added to norepinephrine/vasopressin) | Hypotension, tachycardia, arrhythmias, ischemia, tachyphylaxis | Start 0.5–1 mcg/kg/min; max 20–40 mcg/kg/min |
| **Milrinone** (inodilator) | PDE-3 inhibitor: ↑CO, ↓PVR and SVR | Cardiogenic shock; **RV failure** | Hypotension, arrhythmias, ischemia (renally cleared) | Start 0.125 mcg/kg/min; max 0.75 mcg/kg/min |
| **Isoproterenol** (chronotrope) | β1 = β2: ↑HR, ↓SVR | Symptomatic bradycardia; Mg-refractory torsades | Hypotension, tachycardia, arrhythmias, ischemia, flushing, anxiety | 2–10 mcg/min (may bolus 2–6 mcg first); max 30 mcg/min |
