ABOUT FOLIO

A place to find your place.

A folio is a leaf in a book. The name reflects the idea behind this reader: a familiar manual, opened to the part you need.

Folio organizes the general medicine content of the MGH Department of Medicine Housestaff Manual, July 2025–June 2026, edited by Christopher Radcliffe, MD and Natalia Sutherland, MD, with its original contributors. Marc Machaalani created the reader interface; the source authors retain credit for the medical content.

Clinical wording and citations are rebuilt as selectable, reflowing text, headings, lists and HTML tables. Original ECGs, imaging examples and clinical diagrams remain figures. Institution-specific phone numbers, routing instructions, order sets and local protocol units are omitted. The omission count for each entry is visible. Retained legal or regional material keeps its original geographic scope.

This is a dated educational reference, not an updated guideline or a validated clinical system. It is independent of, and not endorsed by, MGH. Check current primary sources and applicable local policy before clinical use.

No patient information should be entered. Search, reading-list selections and interface state stay in memory and clear on reload. There are no accounts, analytics or submitted forms.
