ABOUT FOLIO

A place to find your place.

A folio is a leaf in a book. This reader brings a familiar medicine manual into a format that is easier to search, browse and read on any screen.

Created by Marc Machaalani, Folio pairs a compact system index with readable sections, interactive tables and a reading list for your current session.

The medical text retains the book’s wording and citations. Institution-specific contacts and workflows are omitted; retained regional or legal material keeps its geographic scope. This is a dated educational reference, not a validated clinical tool or an updated guideline. Check current guidance and local policy before clinical use.

Searches and reading-list selections stay in memory and clear on reload. There are no accounts, analytics or submitted forms. Do not enter patient information.
